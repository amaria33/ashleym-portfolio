ATS Resume Checklist + Bullet Formula

Use this before you apply. It won't rewrite your resume for you — but it will catch the mistakes that get good candidates filtered out.

1. ATS formatting checklist

  • One column layout (no tables, text boxes, or multi-column designs)
  • Standard section headers: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills
  • Common fonts only (Calibri, Arial, Garamond, Georgia)
  • No headers/footers for contact info — put it in the body
  • File named clearly: FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf
  • Also keep an editable Word (.docx) version
  • Avoid icons, charts, skill bars, and headshots
  • Dates and job titles on the same visual line as the employer when possible

2. Keyword match (5-minute scan)

  • Paste the job description into a doc and highlight required skills
  • Mirror exact phrases when they-re true for you (e.g. -stakeholder management-)
  • Put the top 6-8 skills in a Skills section near the top
  • Use the job title (or closest true title) in your summary
  • Don-t keyword-stuff - every skill should show up in a bullet or role

3. Bullet formula

Action verb + what you did + how/tools + result (number if possible)

  • Weak: Responsible for customer emails and tickets.

    Strong: Resolved 40+ support tickets weekly in Zendesk, cutting average first-response time by 25%.

  • Weak: Helped with social media and content.

    Strong: Wrote and scheduled 12 LinkedIn posts/month that grew engagement 35% over one quarter.

  • Weak: Worked on team projects and reporting.

    Strong: Built weekly Excel dashboards for a 6-person ops team, reducing manual status updates by ~3 hours/week.

4. Before you hit submit

  • Every bullet starts with a strong verb (Led, Built, Improved, Launched-)
  • At least half your bullets include a metric, scope, or timeframe
  • Summary is 2-3 lines max and aimed at one target role
  • No typos; phone/email/LinkedIn are current
  • You tailored at least the summary + top bullets to this job
